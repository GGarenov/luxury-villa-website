The Villa

Space, light, and a sense of having arrived

Set in its own grounds, this private villa is laid out for easy living: generous rooms, three bedrooms each with its own bathroom, and a pool terrace made for long afternoons outside.

Interiors balance calm neutrals with natural materials—stone, timber, soft linen—so the focus stays on the views and the quality of light through the day. Whether you are here to unwind or to host friends, the flow between indoor and outdoor spaces keeps everything feeling open and unhurried.

The property has been thoughtfully refreshed to modern standards while keeping the character that makes a house feel like a home, not a hotel suite.
