Good to know

Booking & deposit

Secure your dates with a €1,000 deposit, or leave a card on file for a pre-authorisation (similar to car rental) — we will confirm which option applies when you book. The balance is due before arrival as agreed in your confirmation.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 30 days before check-in for a full refund of amounts paid. If you cancel within 30 days of arrival, the deposit (or card hold) is retained unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 15:00 (often earlier — see what's included). Check-out by 11:00 unless late check-out has been arranged with your host.
